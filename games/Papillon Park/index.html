Papillon Park

Web Game

Release date: 14 April 2025

Summary: A teaching tool and educational mobile app that teaches students about wildlife through exploration.

A screenshot of the mobile app's AR animal photography function.

Ann is an artist and community organiser excited by heartfelt creative projects. In particular, they are passionate about comics, zines, 2D game art and UI, and illustration— as long as it involves drawing and/or telling stories! Tales that are dramatic, tragic and romantic are what they are most drawn to, with a sprinkling of hope and heart.
