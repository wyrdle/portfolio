Noble Steed Games

Graphic Design / Game Art / Marketing

Summary: Noble Steed Games is an indie game studio and consultancy. I worked as the sole graphic designer for the studio, responsible for all things visual in-house and for client work.

Noble Steed Games' Website.

Rebranding and Website Redesign

In 2022, Noble Steed Games rebranded from No Moss Studios. This required a fresh new look, logo and website, which I spearheaded the conception and execution of. After numerous iterations and back and forths with the team, we settled on the current brand logo and new website design, hoping to capture the fun but also nobility of the studio's work ethic. Functional but friendly and fun was the overall ethos in designing for Noble Steed Games, and this has carried throughout the company's other marketing.

Social Media Content

On-brand graphics for social posts created in multiple formats. Social posts varied from promoting blog content, new events and overall studio updates. These were resized for Instagram, LinkedIn, X and Bluesky.

Motion Graphics / Video Content

For special events that required a deck, animated slide decks were created to have an "idle" screen for viewers. In addition, I animated the motion graphics and edited the videos for all of the studio's long-form and short form content by the studio director.

Slide Decks / Pitch Decks

I was responsible for designing official slide deck templates for internal and external use. These were applied to Culture Books, pitch decks with clients, and any public talks.

Business Card Illustrations and Design

Team businesscards were themed after the tarot deck, with each team member owning a copy of their own arcana. This was to make the cards "collectible", encouraging people to chat and get to know all team members.

Team Illustrations

Icons of the team members used for the website as well as in official documents.

Blog Graphics

Blog covers and in-text spot illustrations were designed in-brand.
