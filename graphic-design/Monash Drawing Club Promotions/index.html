Monash Drawing Club Promotions

Summary: A short series of advertisements for the Monash Drawing Club, optimised for their Instagram account. Made using Adobe Illustrator, AfterEffects and Audition.

A snippet of the original animation.

Each short features fun reasons why students should sign up to the club. Specifically, that members could learn new things, make friends, and struggle through drawing together. Designed to be short, snappy and fun, these were made to be a little humorous.

I wanted to dial up the stylisation of the artists featured in these shorts, adding more moving patterns and texture overlays that any other animation. In addition, this was my first foray into animation vector-drawn characters, controlling their expressions and movements in more detail. The head squish animation in particular required some frame to fram animation to look natural!
