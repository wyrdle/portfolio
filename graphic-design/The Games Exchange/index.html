The Games Exchange

Landing page and Event Graphics

Summary: The Games Exchange is an event series where Australian indie games are traded with international ones, to be showcased on both sides. I was responsible for the event's landing page, and all other visual graphics for the event.

Social Posts

Of all of my work however, I am proudest of the event promotions done for the Games Exchange and WIP Night. These are extensions of the typical business activities the studio does, and I view them as truly exemplifying what we have to offer as a studio— that we are games industry and community leaders. Aside from that, it was exciting for these event promotional graphics to be shared widely online, as well as enjoyed onsite of the events themselves.

Bunting, flyers and on-site phamplets

In addition to the branding and website design, I also took on the role of creating all of Noble Steed Games' social media content, event promotions, slide decks, letterhead templates, and other official documents that required branding. Since being a part of this small team, I have made graphics for all sorts of mediums, digital and print.

Noble Steed Games Website

Landing Page

In 2022, Noble Steed Games rebranded from No Moss Studios. This required a fresh new look, logo and website, which I spearheaded the conception and execution of. After numerous iterations and back and forths with the team, we settled on the current brand logo and new website design, hoping to capture the fun but also nobility of the studio's work ethic. Functional but friendly and fun was the overall ethos in designing for Noble Steed Games, and this has carried throughout the company's other marketing.
